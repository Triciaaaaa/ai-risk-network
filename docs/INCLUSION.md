# 收录标准 / Inclusion Policy

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

这份标准说明什么可以进入公开图谱，以及如何修正或删除。

### 收什么

- **公共人物与机构：**只收具有公开身份的人物，以及公开运作的机构、项目、基金、实验室、媒体或论坛。
- **与 AI 风险直接相关的公开事实：**包括公开任职、成员关系、创办、顾问或董事角色、资助与投资、合著，以及有原文可核查的公开表态。
- **有分量的新人物：**新增人物应有持续、清楚的公共活动，且与 AI 风险议题有直接关系。只有零散互动、低活跃账号或无法核实身份的普通账号不收。
- **可核查的关系：**每条关系至少有一个直接支持该事实的公开来源。来源应让读者看出人物、机构、关系和必要的时间信息。

论坛作者按公开活跃度门槛收录。笔名只有在账号或本人公开页面明确对应时才与实名合并；否则保留公开笔名。

### 不收什么

- 邮箱、电话、住址、即时通信账号或其他联系方式。
- 私信、私人文件、未公开申请、非公开会议内容或推测。
- 家庭、伴侣等私人关系。
- 没有独立公共身份的非公众人物。
- “谁认识谁”、可能的引荐路径、共同出席但没有公开关系依据的连线。
- 仅凭阵营印象、传闻或算法相似度推断的关系。

### 阵营与社区怎么写

- 标签只陈述来源可核查的事实，例如“签署《X》”“公开支持某项提案”或“公开反对某项政策”。
- 不使用“好／坏”“可信／不可信”“温和／激进”等评价型阵营标签。
- 不把任职、合著、同场出现或获得同一资助自动解释为立场一致。
- 结构社区只使用 `C01`、`C02` 等中性编号，并标明“实验”。编号表示图结构中的聚类，不代表意识形态、关系亲密度或项目评价。

### 更正与删除

**事实更正：**提交 [fix-request 表单](https://github.com/Triciaaaaa/ai-risk-network/issues/new?template=fix-request.yml)或 pull request。请指出具体节点或关系、现有内容、建议改法和公开来源。维护时会保留可核查部分，修正或删除缺乏支持的部分。

**当事人删除：**被收录者本人或其代理可提交 [remove-request 表单](https://github.com/Triciaaaaa/ai-risk-network/issues/new?template=remove-request.yml)，也可以开普通 issue。删除请求无条件处理；原因可不填写。处理范围可以是整个节点，也可以是指定关系。删除节点时会一并移除与它相连的关系。

公开 issue 不适合放身份证明或私人材料。表单只需要说明“本人”或“代理”，以及要删除的节点或关系。

### 免责声明

收录只表示发现了符合标准的公开信息。它不代表认可、反对、可信度判断、风险评级或重要性排名。分类和结构社区是浏览工具，不是对个人或机构的评价。

## English

This policy explains what belongs in the public graph and how records are corrected or removed.

### What is included

- **Public people and organisations.** Include people with a public role and publicly operating organisations, projects, funds, labs, media outlets, or forums.
- **Public facts directly related to AI risk.** These include public roles, membership, founding, advisory or board positions, funding and investment, co-authorship, and public positions that readers can verify in the original source.
- **Substantial new people.** A new person should have a clear, sustained public record directly connected to AI-risk topics. Exclude low-activity accounts, isolated interactions, and ordinary accounts whose identities cannot be verified.
- **Verifiable relations.** Every relation needs at least one public source that directly supports the people or organisations involved, the relation, and any necessary date.

Forum authors are included under published activity thresholds. A pseudonym is merged with a named person only when the account or the person's own public page makes the identity explicit; otherwise the public pseudonym is retained.

### What is excluded

- Email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, messaging accounts, and other contact details.
- Private messages, private files, unpublished applications, non-public meeting content, and speculation.
- Family, partner, and other private relationships.
- Non-public people without an independent public role.
- “Who knows whom,” possible introduction paths, or links based only on appearing at the same event.
- Relations inferred only from perceived camps, rumours, or algorithmic similarity.

### Language for positions and communities

- Use labels that state source-backed facts, such as “signed ‘X’,” “publicly supported a proposal,” or “publicly opposed a policy.”
- Do not use evaluative camp labels such as good/bad, trustworthy/untrustworthy, or moderate/radical.
- Do not treat employment, co-authorship, appearing at the same event, or receiving the same funding as automatic agreement.
- Structural communities use neutral identifiers such as `C01` and `C02` and are marked “experimental.” They describe graph clusters, not ideology, closeness, or project evaluation.

### Corrections and removals

**Factual corrections:** submit the [fix-request form](https://github.com/Triciaaaaa/ai-risk-network/issues/new?template=fix-request.yml) or a pull request. Identify the node or relation, the current problem, the proposed change, and a public source. Verifiable parts are preserved; unsupported parts are corrected or removed.

**Removal by a represented person:** the person shown in the graph or their agent may use the [remove-request form](https://github.com/Triciaaaaa/ai-risk-network/issues/new?template=remove-request.yml) or open a blank issue. Removal requests are honoured without conditions, and no reason is required. A request may cover a full node or selected relations. Removing a node also removes its connected relations.

Do not put identity documents or private material in a public issue. The form asks only whether the requester is the represented person or an agent, and which node or relation should be removed.

### Disclaimer

Inclusion means only that public information met this policy. It is not endorsement, opposition, a credibility judgment, a risk rating, or an importance ranking. Categories and structural communities are browsing aids, not evaluations of people or organisations.
